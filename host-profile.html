About Dr. Maya Carter

Dr. Maya Carter has dedicated the past eight years to companion animal nutritional biochemistry and individualized dietary formulations. She completed her graduate degree in Animal Science with clinical research in canine gastrointestinal microbiomes and food allergen responses.

Rather than prescribing one-size-fits-all commercial diets, Maya partners with pet parents to design biologically appropriate meals using whole, wholesome food sources, targeted micronutrient balances, and precise caloric pacing.

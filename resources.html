Daily Gram & Calorie Guidelines

Most feeding guidelines printed on commercial bags overestimate calories by 20% to 30%, which contributes directly to pet obesity. Use our clinical resting energy formula as a baseline:

RER = 70 × (Body Weight in kg)0.75
Resting Energy Requirement (RER) is then adjusted by activity multiplier (1.2 to 1.6 for neutered adults).

Key Label Red Flags

Unspecified Animal Fats: Labels stating simply "animal fat" rather than "chicken fat" or "salmon oil" indicate variable rendering sources.
Ingredient Splitting: Listing "peas, pea starch, pea flour" separately allows fresh meat to artificially appear first on the ingredient list.
AAFCO Adequacy Statement: Look for "Formulated to meet the nutritional levels established by the AAFCO Nutrient Profiles" rather than "For intermittent feeding only".

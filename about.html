Our Purpose

Better Nutrition. Happier Pets.

NOURIPET was built to solve the frustration pet parents face when seeking trustworthy, science-led dietary guidance for their dogs and cats.

Our Mission

Bridging Clinical Nutrition & Conscientious Pet Care

For decades, companion animal nutrition was treated as an afterthought—dominated by opaque marketing claims, mystery meat meals, and generic feeding tables printed on the back of kibble sacks.

NOURIPET creates a direct bridge between pet parents and independent, verified animal nutritionists, clinical dietitians, and formulation specialists. We believe tailored nutrition is the single most powerful preventative health tool available for our companions.

The Platform Model

How The Marketplace Works

A frictionless ecosystem for discovery, real-time booking, and escrow-protected deposits.

1

Pet Parent Discovery

Filter nutrition services by species, health condition (e.g. allergies, renal, weight), duration, and modality. Review transparent pricing and verified client testimonials.

2

Host Professional Hub

Qualified animal nutrition specialists host their clinical services, publish live calendar schedules, and set upfront rates without middlemen commissions.

3

Escrow Security

A partial $25 deposit locks in the appointment slot on the specialist's calendar. The remaining balance is released only once the consultation has taken place.

Ethical Standards

Trust & Transparency

We uphold strict standards across all marketplace participants to ensure evidence-led advice without commercial bias.

No Brand Kickbacks
Our hosts do not receive covert brand commissions for food recommendations.
Academic Rigor
Dietary recommendations follow NRC, FEDIAF, and AAFCO nutritional boundaries.
Zero Risk Reschedules
Change your appointment anytime up to 24 hours prior with full deposit retention.
Vet Coordination
We encourage sharing consultation PDF plans with your primary veterinarian.

Service Overview

Does your companion suffer from unexplained itchy skin, dull coat, digestive irregularity, or fluctuating energy? Most commercial pet foods are designed for average pets, but your dog is an individual with specific metabolic, breed-level, and environmental demands.

This comprehensive 60-minute consultation is a diagnostic deep dive led by certified canine clinical nutritionist Dr. Maya Carter. We evaluate your pet’s complete health history, inspect current food labels, analyze recent blood panels or veterinary notes, and construct a targeted nutritional roadmap.

What You'll Get

Itemized Dietary Audit
Nutrient-by-nutrient breakdown of protein bioavailability, essential fatty acids, and calcium-phosphorus ratio.
Custom Caloric Target
Metabolic energy calculations tailored to your dog’s age, neutered status, and activity level.
Allergen Elimination Plan
Targeted 6-week single-protein trial protocol to isolate itchy hotspots and digestive flare-ups.
Actionable PDF Summary
Delivered to your email within 24 hours of the live session for reference and vet coordination.

Who It's For

  • Dogs with chronic scratching, ear inflammation, yeast, or paw licking.
  • Dogs recovering from pancreatitis, renal stress, or recurring loose stool.
  • Pet guardians transitioning from ultra-processed kibble to fresh or gently-cooked diets.
  • Picky eaters requiring palatability boosting without digestive upset.

How to Prepare for Your Session

  1. Take clear photos of the ingredient panel and guaranteed analysis of all foods, treats, and supplements your pet currently ingests.
  2. Gather any recent veterinary diagnostic records or blood panels from the past 6 months (optional but encouraged).
  3. Note down specific feeding times, daily portion sizes in grams or cups, and symptoms timeline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Upon confirming your booking and deposit, you receive an automated calendar invitation with a private Zoom video link. We conduct the consultation live, screen-share nutritional analysis tools, and answer all questions in real-time.

To respect our specialists' time, a $25 deposit is authorized upon booking. The remaining balance ($50) is only settled when the consultation session concludes.

Our nutritionists are certified clinical dietitians and animal nutritionists. We provide dietary, supplementary, and metabolic meal formulations. We work in tandem with your primary veterinarian, but do not prescribe pharmaceutical drugs.
